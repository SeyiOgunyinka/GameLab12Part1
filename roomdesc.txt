Living Room 
The small living room contains a couch, coffee table, and two armchairs.
Pictures of various cousins adorn the walls along with two of your aunt's wretched attempts
at oil painting. You can't help but notice that the carpet could use a good vacuuming.
Doorways lead east to the Kitchen, west to the Office, and north to the Bedroom.
#

Bedroom 
This is your domain and has been since your family moved here when you were six.
A nightstand with a Superman lamp sits next to your twin bed.
Posters of various anime characters cover one wall. A pile of laundry,
slowly but surely attaining sentience, occupies the far corner.
You can return south to the Living Room or go up to the Attic.
#

Attic 
A dusty attic filled with old cardboard boxes, forgotten furniture, 
and belongings that have not been touched in years. Dim light shines through a small window, 
revealing cobwebs in the corners and dust covering the wooden floor.
You can only go down to the Bedroom.
#
Garage
The garage contains parked cars, tools stored along the walls, and shelves
filled with supplies. A workbench sits in the corner with equipment scattered across 
its surface.
You can only go north to the Kitchen.
#

Kitchen
The kitchen has countertops, cabinets, a refrigerator, and a stove.
Cooking utensils hang near the counter, while a sink sits beneath a window overlooking 
the outside.
You can go west to the Living Room or south to the Garage.
#
Office
The quiet office has a desk, a comfortable chair, and shelves filled with books and
study materials. Papers are arranged neatly across the desk, and a lamp provides light for
reading and working.
You can only go east to the Living Room.
#
